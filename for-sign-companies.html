For sign companies

You keep the client.
We fabricate the letters.

ALFA is a factory in Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh. We make stainless steel, aluminium and acrylic 3D letters, halo-lit and front-lit logos, LED marks and neon flex for sign companies that want overflow capacity or a white-label line — not a competitor on their street.

How the job runs. You send artwork and sizes. We confirm drawings. We fabricate, pack for export, and ship EXW / FOB India. Your crew installs. We do not pitch your end client.

What we make

  • SS 304 and aluminium built-up letters
  • Acrylic front-lit and reverse / halo-lit letters
  • LED logos and tray signs
  • Neon flex (LED), not glass
  • Basic wayfinding panels on request
